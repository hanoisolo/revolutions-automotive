    Ontario Safety Standards Certificate (MTO SSC)

    Buying, selling or registering a used vehicle, or joining a rideshare platform? Ontario requires a Safety Standards Certificate before registration. We perform the full MTO inspection — brakes, steering, suspension, tires, lighting, windshield and exhaust — and issue the certificate on a pass. Mention coupon SAFETY10 to save $10.

    • Used-vehicle pre-purchase compliance inspections
    • Rideshare and taxi licensing inspections
    • Exhaust system checks included
